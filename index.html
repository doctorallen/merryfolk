      Merryfolk

      You are the Steward of Thistledown, a snug village of hobbit-holes, gardens and good ale between green hills and a slow river. Its folk live their own lives: they work, fall in love, raise families and, one day, set off over the hills. Lately the sky has begun to tear open, and neon-lit strangers are stepping through.

      Shape itBuild paths, gardens, lamps, ponds and new homes. Your folk do the work.
