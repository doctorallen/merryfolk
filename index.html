    Merryfolk

    Thistledown is a snug village of hobbit-holes, gardens and good ale, tucked between green hills and a slow river. Its folk are born, grow up, go to school, take up trades, fall in love, raise families and, one day, set off over the hills. Lately, though, the sky has begun to tear open, and strange neon-lit folk are stepping through. You can watch over them all and guide them a little.

    Look aroundDrag the map, or use WASD / arrow keys. Scroll or +− to zoom.
    Get to know folkClick a villager to open their panel: needs and health, genes, habits and education, family and friends, trade and skills. Tab cycles through everyone.
    Teach themClick a selected villager again (or P) to pat them, and they'll do more of whatever they were doing. T tut-tuts. E gives a lesson: little ones learn fastest, and education shapes how folk talk, work and spend their days.
    The giant handDrag a villager to pick them up and carry them somewhere. Little ones love it; grown-ups less so.
    Gifts & remediesDrop cake, ale, leaf, storybooks, remedies, flowers or fireworks from the toolbar. Fireworks in the party field start a party.
